# WordChord Spark Challenges

WordChord Spark Challenges are weekly creative prompts exclusive to WordChord users. Each Monday at 09:00 UTC a new challenge is published inside the app under the **Discover** tab (lightning bolt icon in the navigation bar).

## How It Works

1. Open the **Discover** tab and read the week's prompt — for example "Write a verse using only three chords" or "Finish a song that starts on a minor chord."
2. Create or pick a song in your library that fits the prompt.
3. Click **Submit to Spark** inside the song editor. The song title and your username are shared publicly; the song content stays private unless you opt in to share it.
4. At the end of the week (Sunday 23:59 UTC) the WordChord team picks three winners.

## Spark Ranks

Every submission earns Spark Points regardless of winning:

- Submission: 10 points
- Peer vote received: 2 points per vote (max 50 votes counted)
- Weekly winner: 200 points

Accumulated points unlock Spark Ranks: **Ember** (0–199), **Flame** (200–499), **Blaze** (500–1499), **Inferno** (1500+). Your current rank is shown next to your username on the Friends page.

## Prizes

Weekly winners receive a **Spark Badge** on their profile and early access to new WordChord features before public release. There is no cash prize. Badges are permanent and cannot be removed.

## Rules

- Each user may submit one song per challenge week.
- Songs must be original — covers of existing songs are not eligible.
- The challenge resets every Monday regardless of your time zone.
- Voting opens Thursday 09:00 UTC and closes Sunday 18:00 UTC.
