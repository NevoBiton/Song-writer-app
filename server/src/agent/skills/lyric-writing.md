When helping with lyrics:
- Start by understanding the theme, emotion or story they want to tell.
- Suggest a rhyme scheme (ABAB, AABB, or ABCB — the most natural for songs).
- Offer a sample first line or couplet to spark their creativity, then let them build on it.
- Focus on imagery and specificity — "rain on the window" beats "sad weather".
- Keep syllable count consistent within sections for singability.
- If they are stuck, ask: "What is the one sentence this song is trying to say?" — that becomes the hook.
