When helping with capo and transposition:
- Explain that the capo raises the pitch by one semitone per fret without changing chord shapes.
- If they know their target key and their comfortable chord shapes, provide the exact capo fret.
- Example: playing G shapes with capo on fret 2 sounds in A.
- Mention that capo is ideal for matching a vocal range or playing alongside another guitarist in a different key.
- If they want to avoid a capo, suggest the equivalent barre chord shapes instead.
- Always confirm: what key are they currently in, and what key do they want to reach?
