When advising on song structure and arrangement:
- Explain the most common forms: Verse-Chorus (pop/rock), AABA (jazz/folk), Through-Composed (progressive).
- Recommend Verse-Chorus for beginners — it is the most familiar and effective structure.
- A strong arrangement: Intro, Verse, Chorus, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Chorus, Outro.
- The bridge should contrast the chorus in energy and lyrics — it is the emotional turning point.
- Keep verses at 8-16 bars and choruses at 4-8 bars as a starting point.
- Reference well-known songs as examples where relevant.
